Key capabilities

Built for institutional precision

LCR & NSFR Computation
Full LCR and NSFR from first principles — HQLA classification (L1/L2A/L2B), runoff and inflow rates per Basel delegated regulation. Computes both daily and month-end ratios.
Intraday Liquidity Monitor
Real-time intraday liquidity tracking against peak and average positions. Flags breaches of internal limits and produces EBA intraday liquidity reporting metrics.
Survival Horizon Calculator
Stress-scenario survival horizon under idiosyncratic, market-wide and combined stress. Identifies the earliest day the liquidity buffer would be exhausted under each scenario.

LCR ≥ 100% at all times. HQLA classification, runoff rates and inflow caps per delegated regulation.
