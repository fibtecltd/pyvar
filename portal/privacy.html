Privacy Policy

What pyvar.com collects, why, how long it's kept, and what Google Sign-In actually shares. Last updated: November 2026.

pyvar.com is a product of Fibtec Limited, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 07098379, with registered office at 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ (“Fibtec”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal data pyvar.com collects, why, and what your rights are. Fibtec is the data controller for your account data.

1. What pyvar.com actually stores about a computation

pyvar.com is a deterministic risk-computation engine, not an AI product — results are produced by a Numba-JIT-compiled Monte Carlo/statistical kernel running on our own infrastructure, never by sending your data to a third-party AI model. This matters for what's below, because it means there's nothing beyond what's described here that touches your computation inputs.

A request to compute a risk metric (e.g. Value-at-Risk) typically includes a portfolio value, a series of historical returns (plain numbers — no instrument names, tickers, or counterparty identities are part of this API), and parameters like confidence level and horizon. The historical-returns series itself is used only in memory, for the single computation that needs it, and is never written to a database or object store. What we do keep, as a permanent audit record (see §4), is the job's metadata: your account identifier, the scalar parameters you submitted (portfolio value, confidence level, horizon, simulation count), and the resulting risk metrics — not the raw return series that produced them.

2. Personal data we collect about your account

pyvar.com has one kind of account — there's no separate organisation/company concept, and sign-up asks for no name and no physical address:

  • Your email address — your account identity. Any email address is accepted, including personal ones (no password required to sign up, and no payment card either). You need to give an e-mail address to have an account; without one we cannot create it. Education accounts are held to the free limits automatically when the shared education allowance runs out; nothing else about your account is decided by automated means.
  • Authentication data — pyvar.com uses no password at all. You sign in with an emailed single-use link or Google Sign-In (see §3), which gives a short-lived (60-minute) signed JSON Web Token (JWT) used to manage your API keys. An API key is a random secret shown to you once; we store only its one-way SHA-256 hash, a name you choose, a short non-secret prefix so you can recognise it, and its creation, expiry, last-used and revocation times. We cannot recover a lost key; you create a new one and revoke the old. We hold a per-account identifier used to validate tokens and keys, not a password of any kind.
  • Billing and usage information, only if you upgrade to a paid tier — see §6.
  • Education access, only if you apply for it.
    • Your application. You apply by e-mail to info@pyvar.com, so we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write: usually your name, your role, your institution and the course, and a line confirming that you are 18 or over. Please send no more than that. Do not send identity documents, student or staff cards, or anything about your health, beliefs, ethnic background, politics, trade union membership or private life, and do not send other people's details. We do not need any of it. If you send it anyway, we delete that message and ask you to write again without it. You don't have to apply, but without this we can't consider you for education access; the free tier needs none of it. A person reads the application and decides; no part of the decision is automated. The e-mail stays in our company mailbox (see §9) and is not copied into the pyvar.com database. Whether we grant or refuse, we delete the e-mail within two months of our decision and keep no note of it. An extension is a new request, and the same rule applies to it. Backup copies kept by our e-mail provider are outside our control and expire on its own schedule.
    • What the database holds. Your account's tier is set to education. Each change (granted, extended, ended early or paused) is recorded against your account identifier with the date, the tier before and after, and a short label we choose for your course or cohort. Nothing you wrote to us is put there. We keep each of these records for six years from its date and then delete it. Six years is the time within which a legal claim about our Terms can normally be brought, and we keep the record so that we can show what access an account had and when. After your access ends, your account keeps the label “education” with its end date for up to 12 months and then goes back to plain “free”. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your e-mail address and switch off your API keys; the records of tier changes then carry only an account identifier that we can no longer match to you, and they are deleted when they reach six years. While your access is active, the job record (§1) and our usage statistics show the tier as education; the usage statistics carry no account identifier.
    • Other records. We e-mail you when access is granted, extended, ended early or paused, through the same service that sends sign-in links (§9). Our server logs note that such an e-mail was sent for your account, with the domain of your address but not the address itself, and are kept for about a month.
    • Why, and on what basis. We read your application, decide on it and reply because you have asked us to (Article 6(1)(b) of the UK GDPR: steps taken at your request before a contract). Once we give you access, we keep your account's tier and end date and send you e-mails about it to provide the access under our Terms (Article 6(1)(b): performance of a contract). We keep the record of each change, check how many grants one institution already has, and keep your application for up to two months after we decide, because of our legitimate interests in stopping a free grant being misused and in being able to show why an account had higher limits (Article 6(1)(f)). We have weighed those interests against yours, in writing. You can object to the processing that rests on Article 6(1)(f); we will then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds to carry on, and we will tell you what they are. You can also ask us at any time to delete your application, or for a copy of what we hold about you (see §8). We do not use any of it for marketing.

Why we use it, and on what basis. We use your e-mail address, your sign-in data and your API keys to give you the account and the service you signed up for (performance of our contract with you). We keep the job record, tier-change records and server logs to run the service safely, prevent misuse and be able to show what was done (our legitimate interests). We use billing data to take payment and keep the accounts the law requires (contract, and legal obligation). We do not use your data for marketing and we do not sell it.

3. Google Sign-In — what it shares, and what it doesn't

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your email address and whether Google considers it verified, which we use to either create a new pyvar.com account or sign you into an existing one matched by that email address. Unlike some other Fibtec products, Google Sign-In on pyvar.com can create a brand-new account directly — it isn't restricted to linking an account that already exists, because pyvar.com has no domain-matching or organisation-membership rule for Google to conflict with.

We request only the standard, minimal Google identity scope (your email address and whether it's verified) — nothing about your contacts, files, or any other Google data. Google's own ID token is verified entirely on our servers; it is never forwarded to any third party.

4. How long computation and account records are kept

We keep your e-mail address, your account and your API key records for as long as your account is open. If you ask us to close it, we delete your e-mail address and switch off your API keys within one month; the account itself stays as an identifier with no e-mail address. Records of tier changes are kept for six years from their date, and so are job records.

The job audit record described in §1 (account identifier, submitted parameters, resulting metrics — never the raw return series) is kept for six years from its date as a compliance audit trail, consistent with standard practice for regulatory risk-computation records, and then deleted.

The full computed result (the complete loss-distribution output, not just the summary metrics stored in the audit record) is kept in object storage for a bounded period — currently up to 90 days in our standard environment, and up to 365 days where a longer compliance-retention window applies — after which it is deleted automatically. The summary metrics in the audit record above are unaffected by this deletion.

Our application logs, kept for about one month, carry the domain of your e-mail address, not the address itself. The network layer in front of the service (Amazon CloudFront) keeps access logs, which include the IP address and the address requested, for 90 days. We also use the IP address of a request to limit abuse, and keep it in our cache for up to about a day.

5. No AI processing of your computation data

To be explicit, since pyvar.com is also distributed as Claude Code plugins and an MCP server: when you choose to call pyvar.com's API through Claude Code or Claude.ai, whatever you type into Claude is a conversation between you and Anthropic, governed by Anthropic's own privacy terms — pyvar.com only ever sees the resulting API call, exactly as if you'd made it yourself with curl. pyvar.com's own backend never sends your portfolio data, return series, or computed results to Anthropic or any other AI provider; our compute kernel is not an AI model.

6. Payment data

If you upgrade to Pro, payment is handled entirely by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number, expiry date, security code or billing address — Stripe's own hosted checkout collects them directly. We store a reference to your Stripe customer, your tier, and a record of each change to it with the reference of the Stripe event that caused it (kept as section 4 says). Your invoices and payment history are in Stripe: the Manage billing button on your dashboard opens them.

7. Cookies and tracking

pyvar.com does not use cookies, and we do not run any analytics or advertising tracking — no Google Analytics, no advertising pixels. Your session token (JWT) is kept in your browser's local storage, used only to authenticate your own API calls back to pyvar.com — never for tracking, profiling, or advertising, and never read by anyone but your own browser.

8. Your rights, and how to exercise them

pyvar.com does not currently offer self-service account deletion through the product itself. If you want your account data deleted, contact us at info@pyvar.com and we will action the request.

Under the UK GDPR and, where it applies to you, the EU GDPR, you have the right to access, correct or request erasure of your personal data, to object to or restrict our processing of it, and to data portability. Write to info@pyvar.com to use any of these rights; we reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first at the same address (see below). You can also complain to the UK data protection regulator (ico.org.uk). If you live or work in the EU, you can instead complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

Complaints. If you think we have handled your personal data wrongly, write to info@pyvar.com with “Data protection complaint” in the subject line. We will acknowledge it within 30 days and tell you the outcome without undue delay. You can also complain to the regulator at any time (see above).
