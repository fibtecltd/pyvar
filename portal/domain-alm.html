Key capabilities

Built for institutional precision

IRRBB Six Standard Rate Shocks
EVE and NII sensitivity across all six BCBS standard rate shock scenarios — parallel up/down, short rate shock, long rate shock, flattener and steepener — in any currency.
Behavioural Modelling (NMDs)
Non-maturity deposit modelling for current accounts, savings accounts and notice deposits. Separates core (stable) from non-core (volatile) and assigns repricing dates per EBA guidance.
Funds Transfer Pricing
Marginal FTP curve construction — assigns a transfer price to each asset and liability based on its liquidity and repricing profile. Supports multi-curve and option-adjusted pricing.
