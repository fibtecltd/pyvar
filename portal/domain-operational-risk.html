Key capabilities

Built for institutional precision

Loss Distribution Approach (LDA)
Fits severity and frequency distributions to internal loss data. Aggregates via FFT or Monte Carlo to produce the compound loss distribution and tail capital estimate at 99.9%.
RCSA Framework
Structured risk and control self-assessment capturing inherent risk, control effectiveness and residual risk scores across business lines and risk categories.
Scenario Analysis
Expert-elicitation model for scenario severity and frequency estimation. Generates plausible tail scenarios for events not well-represented in internal loss history.
