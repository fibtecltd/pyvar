Key capabilities

Built for institutional precision

Basel IV Capital Stack
Full computation of CET1, Tier 1 and Total Capital ratios with all deductions, buffers (CCB, CCyB, G-SII, O-SII) and the new output floor binding constraint.
FRTB SA & IMA
Sensitivity-Based Method for SA: delta, vega and curvature risk charges per asset class. IMA: stressed ES, NMRF capital charge and P&L Attribution Test thresholds.
MiFID II Algorithm Documentation
Auto-generates FCA-compliant algorithm documentation covering decision logic, controls, kill-switch procedures and annual self-assessment per RTS 6.
